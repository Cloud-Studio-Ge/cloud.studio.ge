About the studio

Every film deserves to speak Georgian.

Cloud Studio.ge is a Georgian dubbing studio. We bring movies, series and animation to Georgian audiences with natural translation, carefully cast voices and clean, cinema-quality sound so the story feels like it was made in our language from the start.
